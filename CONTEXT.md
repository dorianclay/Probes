# Probes

Probing LLM internal activations for belief-like states, and measuring how coherent those beliefs are.

## Language

### Beliefs and their sources

**Prevision**:
A value in [0, 1] that the model is taken to assign to a statement being true; the bookie's quoted price for a $1 bet on it.
_Avoid_: prediction, credence, score

**Prevision source**:
Where a prevision is read from — either a **probe prevision** (a trained probe's output on the statement's activations) or an **elicited prevision** (the model's own behavior when asked about the statement).
_Avoid_: method, readout

**Bookie**:
The entity whose previsions are being tested for coherence — here, an LLM viewed through one prevision source.
_Avoid_: agent, model (when the prevision source matters)

### Coherence

**Event family**:
A set of statements with logical relations known by construction (e.g. A, ¬A, B, ¬B, A∧¬B), over which previsions are jointly checked for coherence.
_Avoid_: book, group, cluster

**Atom**:
One cell of the partition generated by an event family — a complete assignment of truth values consistent with its logical structure.
_Avoid_: world, state (in prose)

**Constituent pair**:
One of the two negation pairs a conjunction family is built from. Conjunction families sharing a constituent pair are not independent of each other.
_Avoid_: parent pair, source pair

**Domain**:
One subject area of statements together with its negation and conjunction variants — here, *facts* or *companies*. A probe is trained on one domain (its **training domain**) and books the families of the other (the **booked domain**).
_Avoid_: dataset (a domain spans several dataset files), topic

**Rate of loss**:
The largest guaranteed loss per unit of normalization that a gambler can force on the bookie over an event family; zero exactly when the previsions are coherent. Which normalization is used is stated alongside it.
_Avoid_: incoherence score, Dutch-book-ability, penalty
